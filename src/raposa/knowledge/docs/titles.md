# Major titles

## Copa Libertadores
Cruzeiro won the Copa Libertadores twice: in 1976, beating River Plate in a three-match final, and in 1997, beating Sporting Cristal of Peru. Both times the club then lost the Intercontinental Cup, to Bayern Munich in 1976 and to Borussia Dortmund in 1997.

## Brazilian championship
Cruzeiro has four national championships: the 1966 Taça Brasil (recognised as a Brazilian title) and the Brasileirão in 2003, 2013 and 2014. In the 1966 Taça Brasil final Cruzeiro beat Pelé's Santos 6-2 at the Mineirão in the first leg and won the second leg 3-2 in São Paulo.

## Copa do Brasil
Cruzeiro is the most successful club in the history of the Copa do Brasil, with six titles: 1993, 1996, 2000, 2003, 2017 and 2018.

## The 2003 treble
In 2003 Cruzeiro won the Campeonato Mineiro, the Copa do Brasil and the Brasileirão in the same season, the first Brazilian treble ("Tríplice Coroa"). The team was led by playmaker Alex and coached by Vanderlei Luxemburgo.

## Other continental titles
Cruzeiro also won the Supercopa Libertadores in 1991 and 1992 and the Recopa Sudamericana in 1998. The club reached the 2024 Copa Sudamericana final, losing 3-1 to Racing Club in Asunción.

## Série B
Cruzeiro won the Série B title in 2022, sealing its return to the top flight.
