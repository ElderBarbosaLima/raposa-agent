# Idols

## Tostão and Dirceu Lopes
Forwards Tostão and Dirceu Lopes were the stars of the late-1960s team that won the 1966 Taça Brasil. Tostão went on to win the 1970 World Cup with Brazil.

## The 1976 Libertadores team
The 1976 Libertadores side included goalkeeper Raul Plassmann, captain Wilson Piazza, right-back Nelinho, forward Palhinha and winger Joãozinho, who scored the decisive free kick in the final play-off against River Plate.

## Ronaldo
Ronaldo Nazário made his professional debut for Cruzeiro in 1993 as a teenager and won the 1993 Copa do Brasil before moving to PSV Eindhoven in 1994. He later became the controlling shareholder of the club's SAF.

## Alex
Playmaker Alex was the captain and star of the 2003 treble-winning team and is one of the most loved idols in the club's history.

## Fábio
Goalkeeper Fábio holds the club record for most appearances, having played for Cruzeiro for more than fifteen seasons, winning the 2013 and 2014 Brasileirão and the 2017 and 2018 Copa do Brasil.

## The 2013-2014 champions
The back-to-back Brasileirão champions of 2013 and 2014 were coached by Marcelo Oliveira and featured midfielders Everton Ribeiro and Ricardo Goulart.
