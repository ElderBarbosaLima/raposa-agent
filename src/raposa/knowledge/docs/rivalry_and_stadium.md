# Rivalry and stadium

## The Clássico Mineiro
Cruzeiro's great rival is Atlético Mineiro. The derby between them, the Clássico Mineiro, is the biggest match in Minas Gerais and dates back to the 1920s. Fans of both clubs dispute which side leads the all-time head-to-head record, since the count depends on which matches are included.

## Other local rivals
América Mineiro is the third big club of Belo Horizonte.

## The Mineirão
Cruzeiro plays its home matches at the Mineirão (Estádio Governador Magalhães Pinto), opened in 1965 in the Pampulha region of Belo Horizonte. The stadium was renovated for the 2014 FIFA World Cup. The arrival of the Mineirão coincided with the rise of the Tostão and Dirceu Lopes generation.
