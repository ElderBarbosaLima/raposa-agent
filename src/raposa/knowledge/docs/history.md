# Club history

## Founding as Palestra Italia
Cruzeiro was founded on 2 January 1921 in Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais, by members of the city's Italian community, under the name Società Sportiva Palestra Italia. The club wore green, white and red in its early years.

## The name change to Cruzeiro
In 1942, during the Second World War, Brazil restricted names that referred to Axis countries, and Palestra Italia became Cruzeiro Esporte Clube. The new name refers to the Southern Cross (Cruzeiro do Sul), the constellation that also gives the crest its five stars. The club adopted blue and white as its colours.

## Nicknames and mascot
The club is known as "a Raposa" (the Fox), after its fox mascot, and supporters often call it "o Cabuloso". Fans are known as "cruzeirenses".

## Relegation and return
Cruzeiro was relegated from the Brasileirão Série A for the first time in its history at the end of the 2019 season. After two seasons in Série B, the club won the 2022 Série B title and returned to the top flight for 2023.

## Ownership as an SAF
In late 2021 Cruzeiro became a SAF (Sociedade Anônima do Futebol, a football company), with Ronaldo Nazário as the controlling shareholder. In 2024 control of the SAF was sold to businessman Pedro Lourenço.
